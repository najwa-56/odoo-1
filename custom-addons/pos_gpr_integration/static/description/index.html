    Accept Digital Payments Even in Offline Stores
    Impact of online ways has been so much in past years that even when people visit physical stores, they prefer to pay online. You can enhance the experience of your customers by offering them a way to pay online even in the offline store.
    Odoo POS is a perfect tool to sell your products offline as you can manage each and everything perfectly. However, you can add glitter to it and let your customers pay with Geidea Payment Terminal in your Odoo POS Shop.
    Why Choose POS Geidea Payment Terminal Option?
    • Reduces dependence on cash payment.
    • Quick and easy Process for Payments via Geidea Payment Terminal.
    • Same day settlement as you can quickly receive your money into your account.
    • Prone to Less errors and quicker checkout.
    • Secure POS Payments Using Geidea Payment Terminal.
    Need Of Connecting Geidea Payment Terminal to your Odoo PoS
    Connecting a payment terminal facilitates you to provide a fluid payment flow to your customers and ease the work of your cashiers. Odoo helps you to flawlessly serve your customers which also includes hassle-free checkouts.

    Now you can enhance the checkout process of your Odoo Offline shop easily. You just need to integrate your Odoo POS with Geidea Payment Terminal Option. It facilitates you to ask for an easy way of payment from your customers.
